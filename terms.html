the deal

the rules of the doll house, in plain words.

ON THIS PAGE

    TL;DR CYBERDOLL is a concept store. you can browse, wishlist and build a bag, but nothing is actually for sale — yet. no money, no contract, no drama.

    the shop

    Every piece, price, drop and countdown on this site is part of a design concept. The checkout isn't connected to any payment system, so no order can be placed and nothing is ever charged. Adding something to your bag doesn't reserve or sell it.

    photos

    CYBERDOLL is a personal, non-commercial project. Some photos used for moodboards and product mock-ups belong to their original creators. If you see something of yours and want credit or removal, message us and it'll be sorted.

    your Doll ID

    Your Doll ID lives only in your browser. It has no password, so keep it on a device you trust. We can't recover it for you if your browser data is cleared — there's no copy anywhere else.

    points, codes + gift cards

    Doll Points, tiers, promo codes like DOLLCLUB10 and gift cards are part of the concept. They have no cash value and can't be exchanged for anything real.

    be nice

    The comment wall on the about page and the contact chat are saved on your device only — but keep it cute anyway. No hate, no spam.

    changes

    If CYBERDOLL becomes a real shop, these terms will be replaced with proper ones (shipping, returns, your consumer rights, the lot) before anything goes on sale.
