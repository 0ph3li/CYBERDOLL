your data

short version: it never leaves your device.

ON THIS PAGE

    TL;DR we don't have a server. everything you do on CYBERDOLL stays inside your own browser, on your own device. nobody — including us — can see it.

    who we are

    CYBERDOLL is a concept fashion store: a design project, not a registered business. There's no company collecting or processing your data behind this site, and no checkout that takes payments.

    what gets saved, and where

    To make the bag, wishlist and Doll ID work, the site saves small bits of text in your browser's own storage. It never leaves your device.

    whatwherehow long
    your bag, wishlist, notes, comments, recent searches — as a guestsession storageuntil you close the tab
    your Doll ID (nickname, email, avatar, sizes) + everything saved to itlocal storageuntil you delete it
    preferences: units, delivery country, budget slider, grid sizelocal storageuntil you clear it

    You can see every single item, live, on the cookies page.

    about the Doll ID

    Logging in only needs a nickname and an email — there are no passwords, because nothing is ever sent to a server. That also means anyone using the same browser can open your Doll ID by typing your email. On a shared computer, sign out or delete it when you're done.

    what we don't do

    • no analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads
    • no selling or sharing data — there's nothing to sell
    • messages from the contact page aren't sent anywhere yet
    • newsletter + drop sign-up forms don't send or save your email anywhere yet

    other companies

    The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser downloads them, Google receives your IP address, like with any website you visit. The social links in the footer take you to Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest — their own privacy rules apply there.

    you're in control

    Delete your Doll ID any time from account → settings, wipe everything with one click on the cookies page, or clear this site's data from your browser settings.

    changes

    If CYBERDOLL ever opens for real, with real orders, this page will be rewritten properly before anything changes — and you'll see it here first.
