    no cookies, really

    CYBERDOLL doesn't set any cookies, and there's no third-party tracking that would. You'll never be asked to "accept all".

    what we use instead

    Your browser has two little storage boxes that websites can use, and that never get sent anywhere:

    • session storage — wiped when you close the tab. that's why your bag resets each visit when you're not logged in.
    • local storage — stays until you clear it. that's where your Doll ID and preferences live, so logging in brings your stuff back.
