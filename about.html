CYBERDOLL's Blurbs

About me:

CYBERDOLL started on a bedroom floor with a second-hand sewing machine, a pile of thrifted lace and way too many open tabs of Harajuku street snaps and 2004 mall-goth blogs.

We make clothes for girls who live half online and half in the club: corsets that look like error messages, low-rise everything, fur on things that don't need fur. Every drop is a small run. When it's gone, it's gone — we don't restock, we move on to the next obsession.

We think getting dressed is the most fun you can have before midnight, that pretty is a weapon, and that nobody should ever have to choose between cute and dangerous.

Who I'd like to meet:

You, doing your eyeliner on the night bus. You, in the club bathroom fixing a stranger's hair. You, who owns more belts than jeans. Girls who glitch in public. Anyone who ever made a layout at 3am and felt something.
